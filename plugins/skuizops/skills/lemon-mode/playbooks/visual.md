### Visual

The predicate names a screen or a scene and what must be visible. You do not pass it from a test name.

1. Write that predicate before editing. Include the route, the scene path, or the Unity scene, and the thing a viewer can point at.
2. Cherry observes the real artifact: Read the screenshot PNG, or the browser on the URL you started, or a frame of the Unity scene (editor play mode, a built player, or a captured game view). A blank capture, a spinner, the wrong route, a compile, an EditMode test, or a log line is not a PASS. If the artifact cannot be observed, the check is BLOCKED and you stop.
3. Scenes that do not share files may take one cherry each, each brief naming the SHA and the scene. One screen stays one cherry.
4. Out-of-frame problems go to the human list. They do not add a round.
5. Open the PR. The body says what was visible and where the capture is.
